We have seen in this section that there are many different approaches to creating visualisations and assessing those visualisations. We have considered the sorts of rules and principles that might guide us in creating a 'good' visualisation, and thought about when and where these rules might apply, and when we might be able to break them. 

{% panel "prompt" "Principles Summary and Visualisation Assessments" %}
Now it's time to think about how you assess visualisations **personally**. If you come across a visualisation for the first time, how do you approach it? Is there anything you specifically look at/for first? How do you decide whether it's a 'good' visualisation worth looking at further?

Try to write down your initial thoughts on your personal approach to assessing a visualisation, and keep this in mind as we look at further examples in the next few sections.
