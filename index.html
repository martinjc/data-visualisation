Data Visualisation

These notes serve as the base course material for a postgraduate module in Data Visualisation.

They are currently used to create content and learning activities for two modules at Cardiff University:

  • CMT218 - Data Visualisation
  • CMT231 - Data Visualisation in Data Science

Structure

The notes are presented alongside videos covering the main points of the module.

In many notes, there are elements of extra content, like the one below. These will give you more information on the topic currently being discussed and will help you to form a deeper understanding of the topic.

Extra information looks like this

This is an example of extra content, it will not be included in a video summary, but will perhaps help you to go further in a class activity and will certainly help you to do better in one or more of the course assessments. However, should you choose to skip this extra content I will not judge you. Others might.

Some notes will also contain prompts for you to consider. Ideally, in answering these prompts you should stop to consider them and take the time to write down your thoughts.

A Prompt

A prompt will look like this, and may ask some questions about the topic being discussed above. Often these questions will not necessarily have a right or wrong answer, but are asking you to form your own opinions and thoughts on an element of the module. You will get the most out of them by stopping to consider the questions they pose, then writing down your answer.

One of the most important skills you will develop during these modules is the ability to reflect upon and critically evaluate visualisations. One part of developing this skill requires you to read other people’s thoughts and analyses of visualisations. Another part of developing this skill requires you to get used to discussing and writing about visualisations yourself. We will do a lot of discussions during class activities. The prompts within these notes, and the associated notes that you create provide you with a non-assessed opportunity to practice this skill.

Both the assessments for these modules require you to critically evaluate and discuss visualisations. Practicing this now will help with these later assignments.

Occasionally, the notes will contain questions. These simple multiple choice questions allow you to check how well you have understood the material presented. You can skip them if you want to. I won’t tell anyone.
